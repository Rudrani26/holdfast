How Holdfast settles a deposit

Gemini proposes. The shop's price list prices. People approve. PayPal holds and settles the money.
PayPal Sandbox · no real money

Renter

Books and approves the hold in the PayPal popup. Accepts or contests each line from a signed link on their phone.

Shop staff

Takes 6 guided photos at pickup and return. Confirms, re-prices or drops each proposed line.
